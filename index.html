PHESTRA is designed to answer the questions hydrologists and water platforms need in one consistent analytical framework.

01

Early low-flow onset

Identify formation of a new low-flow episode before it becomes obvious in current observations, creating an earlier decision window.

02

Short-horizon high-flow risk

Estimate the probability of crossing a calibrated high-flow level over the coming days as an additional operational risk layer.

03

Current physical state

Convert available observations into a compact, physically interpretable view of the current river or basin state.

04

Trajectory

Track how the system is developing over time instead of treating the latest measurement as an isolated number.

05

WHY

Explain the evidence, current state and forecast path supporting the result so specialists can inspect the logic, not just receive a score.

06

What changed

Turn consecutive analyses into a concise view of the changes that matter most since the previous issue.

07

Prioritize the measurements, state changes and missing inputs most likely to affect the next assessment.

Compose state, risk, explanation, changes and monitoring priorities into one bounded hydrologist-facing summary.

Why PHESTRA

Built for the gap between data platforms and decisions.

Modern platforms already manage data, visualization and model workflows. PHESTRA adds a portable layer for interpretable state, transition intelligence and evidence-bound explanation.

Physical intelligence

Transforms observations and forecast inputs into a physically interpretable view of state, trajectory and operational meaning.

Transition intelligence

Focuses on how a risk state is developing before a threshold event becomes obvious — especially valuable for slow-forming conditions such as low flow.

AI & forecast model compatibility

External AI, ML and hydrological forecasts can be connected as inputs. Models can be replaced or upgraded without rebuilding the PHESTRA intelligence layer.

Fast operational computation

Designed for frequent updates, batch analysis across many objects and operational workflows without a separate heavy research stack.

Platform independence

Connects through APIs and adapters so the intelligence layer can travel across customer environments without forcing a platform replacement.

Flexible deployment

Supports cloud, private-cloud, on-premise and restricted environments to fit different platform and customer security requirements.
